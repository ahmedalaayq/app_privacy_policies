# Terms of Service - Privacy Policy

Welcome to **GDG On Campus Benha**, your gateway to excellence and creativity.

This application is designed to manage the team and its events, including registering students for events and granting them access via a unique code. The core team also uses the app to scan students’ codes, and at the end of each event, it displays a list of all registered students for easy access and complete organization.

## 1. Information Collection

**Personal Data**

When you sign up for a GDGoC Benha account, we collect your email address and any other information you voluntarily provide.

You may be asked to confirm your registration information (such as your email) to continue using the Service. If you choose not to do so, your access to the Service may be restricted or terminated.

After registering, you can optionally upload your personal photo, phone number, and a brief summary about yourself. These fields are not mandatory and will not affect your use of the application.

Camera permissions are required only for the core team to scan students applying to events. No images taken from the camera are stored or used to train AI models. Once a student is scanned, the camera’s role ends, and nothing captured is stored.

When the code is sent to applicants via email, the code image is stored only on the sender’s device because it is sent as an email attachment.

---

## 2. Use of Data

- To provide, maintain, and improve the core features of the app.
- To ensure accurate attendance and registration management for GDG events.

---

## 3. How GDG Benha Protects Your Data

- We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. We retain your information only as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations.
- Users can permanently delete their account data by clicking the "Delete Account" button within the app. This will remove the email, username, password, and any other data linked to the account.
- Your images are **not** used to train AI technologies.
- Users have the right to access, update, and delete their personal information. You can also opt-out of certain features such as push notifications.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

The GDG On Campus Benha app may integrate with or contain links to third-party services such as social media platforms. These third-party services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Users will be notified of changes by posting the updated policy within the app.
