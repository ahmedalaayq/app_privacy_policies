Privacy Policy for Beturn Mobile Application

Last Updated: July 19, 2026

Codeikoo ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Beturn mobile application. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our enterprise resource planning (ERP) and human resource management platform.

By using the Beturn application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

To provide efficient HR and self-service features, our application may require access to specific personal and device data.

A. Location Data (GPS Tracking)

  • Why we collect it: Beturn includes a "Swipe-to-check-in/out" attendance feature. The app accesses your device's geographical location to validate that attendance actions occur within designated company perimeters.
  • Background Location: Location data is captured only at the explicit moment you trigger a check-in or check-out event. We do not track your location continuously in the background.

B. Biometric Data (Fingerprint / Face ID)

  • Why we collect it: For secure and rapid login capabilities.
  • Storage Note: Beturn utilizes native device APIs (local_auth). Your actual biometric blueprints are processed locally by your operating system and are never transmitted to our servers or accessible by Codeikoo.

C. Personal and Employment Data

We store and process employee profile specifics (Names, IDs, employment history, email addresses) and payroll architecture uploaded by your employer to maintain core dashboard services.

2. Data Storage and Security

  • Data Transmission: All communication between the Beturn mobile application and our backend endpoints is encrypted utilizing Secure Socket Layer (SSL/TLS) protocols.
  • Security Measures: We apply industry-standard cloud security infrastructure to protect database information against unauthorized alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

  • Your Employer: All collected attendance data, request logs, and submission records are directly shared with your respective employer's HR administrators.
  • Firebase Services: We use Firebase for crash reporting, telemetry, and delivering reliable push notifications. Firebase processes technical logs anonymized of direct operational context.
  • No Commercial Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing companies.

4. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Since Beturn is an enterprise application managed by your employer, retention periods are subject to company policies and regional labor laws. To request the deletion or adjustment of your account details, please contact your organization's internal HR administrator.
